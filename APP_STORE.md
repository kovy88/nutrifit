# Trenr — App Store Submission Copy

## App Name
Trenr

## Subtitle (30 chars max)
AI Coach for Training & Nutrition

## Description (EN, 4000 chars max)

Trenr gives you one clear answer every morning: train harder, back off, or hold steady — based on how your body actually recovered.

Connect Oura, WHOOP, Garmin, or Apple Health and Trenr reads your sleep, HRV, and resting heart rate overnight. By the time you wake up, your AI coach has already adjusted today's training intensity and your nutrition targets to match your recovery.

**DAILY READINESS SCORE**
See a single score (0–100) the moment you open the app. Green means go. Orange means manage carefully. Red means rest is the workout.

**SMART NUTRITION**
Your calorie and macro targets shift automatically on training days — more carbs before a long run, adjusted protein on rest days. Log food manually or from a photo. The app tracks your streak and 14-day adherence trend so you can see patterns, not just today.

**TRAINING PLAN THAT ADAPTS**
Whether you're training for a half marathon, playing team sports, or just trying to stay consistent — Trenr builds a weekly plan around your goals and adjusts it when your readiness dips.

**AI COACH, ALWAYS ON**
Ask anything: "Why am I tired this week?", "What should I eat before my race?", "Can I add a session tomorrow?" The coach knows your data and answers in plain language.

**WORKS WITH YOUR DEVICES**
- Apple Health (steps, HRV, sleep, heart rate)
- Oura Ring
- WHOOP
- Garmin Connect
- Strava

No device? Trenr still works — manual logging gives you the nutrition and training features from day one.
